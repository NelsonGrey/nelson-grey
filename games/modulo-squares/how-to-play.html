How to play

Guide each square into the right bucket.

Modulo Squares is easy to start because the rule never changes: if the bucket divides the falling number evenly, you score.

01

Read the square

A numbered square drops from the top. Scan its value and the row of buckets below.

02

Choose a lane

Move left or right, or tap Drop, to land the square before time runs out.

03

Score clean division

If the number divides evenly by the bucket, you score. A remainder costs points.

Example landing

18 into 6.

18 % 6 = 0, so this is a clean division and it scores. Land the same square in bucket 4 and 18 % 4 = 2: a remainder, so you lose points.

Bucket 1 is always safe

Avoid Dead
