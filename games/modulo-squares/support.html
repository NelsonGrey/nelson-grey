Support

For bugs, gameplay questions, purchase issues, or anything else about the game, email support@nelsongrey.com. We read every message.

Common questions

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads, or on a new device

Open Settings inside the game and use "Restore Purchases." It reconnects your device to the purchase Apple already has on file — no new charge.

My progress or score didn't carry over to a new device

Your settings and level progress are saved only on your device. Your best score is kept on the Game Center leaderboard, so connect to Game Center (Settings → Game Center in the app) on the new device with the same Apple ID to see it.

A landing was scored wrong

Every falling number has a correct bucket, because bucket 1 is always on the board. If something genuinely looks wrong, email us with the level and the falling number and we'll take a look.

I want to report a bug

Email support@nelsongrey.com with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead — a screenshot helps.
