Register operations

Eight bits. Three ways to move.

The register always tells the truth. Learn what each operation does, combine them deliberately, and find the shortest route to the target.

01 / COUNT

Set the signal.

00010011

Tap cells directly. The lit place values add together to form the register’s number.

02 / SHIFT

Move and multiply.

01001100 → 10011000

Shift left to double. Shift right to halve. Bits crossing an edge are discarded.

03 / ROTATE

Preserve the edge.

10010001 ↻ 11001000

Move every bit while wrapping the edge bit around to the opposite side.
