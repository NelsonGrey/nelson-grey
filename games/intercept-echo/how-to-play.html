Field guide / 001

Learn the register.

Your objective is always visible: transform the eight-bit register until its value matches the target. Intercept Echo introduces each operation before combining them.

01 / COUNT

Build the value

00010011

Tap cells on or off. Each place has a value—128 through 1—and the lit cells add up to your answer.

02 / SHIFT

Move every bit

00100110

Shift left to double; shift right to halve. A bit pushed over an edge disappears, so plan before you move.

03 / ROTATE

Wrap the edge

10001001

Rotate moves every bit too, but the bit at the edge loops around instead of being lost.
