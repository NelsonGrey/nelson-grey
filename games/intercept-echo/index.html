Free puzzle game for iOS

Shift, rotate, crack the code.

Intercept Echo turns an eight-bit register into a fast, tactile puzzle. Shift, rotate, and transform visible bit patterns to match incoming targets before the clock runs out — no prior knowledge of binary or programming required.

How it plays

Learn the register one mechanic at a time.

01

Count

Tap cells to set a value. Simple, exact, no guessing.

02

Shift

Double and halve the register, with bits spilling off the ends as a resource to manage.

03

Rotate

Wrap a bit around instead of losing it. Every operation is deterministic — no luck involved.

Crack the transmission

Work through the Intercept campaign, letter by letter.

Each letter is a register puzzle; solve enough of them and the intercepted message reveals itself. Solve in the fewest moves for the best score — extra moves cost points, so efficient play is rewarded, not just speed.

Scored, not just timed

Fewer moves, higher score — real mastery, not just speed.
