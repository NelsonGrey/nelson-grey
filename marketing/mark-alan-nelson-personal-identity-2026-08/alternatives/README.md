# Alternative concept: Four Bearings

`Four Bearings` is deliberately different from the warmer, family-centered
`Prairie Lineage` direction.

- An open diamond creates a compass-like silhouette without literal compass labels.
- Four directional corners represent Mark's family of four as stabilizing bearings.
- The upward brass point represents education, growth, and purposeful direction.
- Parallel structured paths balance marketing judgment with computer-science rigor.
- A restrained woven turn suggests ancestry without flags, heraldry, or a literal
  Celtic knot.
- The serif `NELSON` emphasizes heritage and permanence; the geometric `MARK ALAN`
  keeps the identity contemporary and personal.
