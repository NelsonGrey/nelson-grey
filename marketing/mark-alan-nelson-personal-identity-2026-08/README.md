# Mark Alan Nelson personal identity

## Concept: Prairie Lineage

This personal identity is related to Nelson Grey LLC through disciplined geometry,
connected paths, warm neutrals, and restrained signal colors. It is deliberately a
different mark rather than a variation of the company logo.

- The navy `M` reads as both an initial and a protective shelter, reflecting Mark's
  role as a husband and father.
- The teal `A` rises through the center as a path of learning and achievement.
- The copper `N` crosses forward as continuity, lineage, and work carried into the
  next generation.
- Four copper waypoints represent the family of four without assigning gender or
  depicting literal people.
- The prairie horizon grounds the mark in Illinois.
- The woven crossings suggest predominantly British Isles and broader European
  ancestry without borrowing flags, heraldry, or school and religious emblems.
- The exact geometry and crossing paths balance Mark's marketing and computer
  science backgrounds without resorting to literal books, diplomas, or code marks.

## Generation prompts

The assets were created with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. The
Nelson Grey brand board was supplied as a style reference only.

### Icon prompt

> Create an original personal emblem for Mark Alan Nelson titled “Prairie
> Lineage.” Build a highly legible, vector-friendly `M A N` monogram as one
> geometric construction. Let the `M` suggest shelter, the `A` an upward path of
> learning and achievement, and the `N` lineage continuing forward. Include
> exactly four balanced waypoint forms for a family of four and an extremely
> subtle Illinois prairie horizon. Use restrained interlacing to suggest British
> Isles and European ancestry without heraldry. Use midnight navy `#142033`,
> prairie teal `#236E70`, burnished copper `#B66A3C`, and warm parchment
> `#F4EFE5`. Flat vector aesthetic, strong 32 px silhouette, transparent outside
> the emblem. Do not trace or copy Nelson Grey's `N`, arc, arrow, nodes, or
> rounded-square construction. No flags, school marks, literal books, computers,
> code brackets, family figures, coats of arms, gradients, shadows, or watermark.

### Horizontal logo prompt

> Preserve the approved Prairie Lineage emblem as the symbol at left and create a
> refined horizontal personal logo with the exact text “MARK ALAN NELSON” at
> right. Use a humanist geometric sans serif with open spacing; keep `MARK` and
> `NELSON` in midnight navy and distinguish `ALAN` in prairie teal. Maintain calm
> precision and generous whitespace while remaining independent of the Nelson
> Grey wordmark. Transparent background, flat colors, no tagline, degrees, `LLC`,
> mockup, shadow, gradient, or extra copy.

## Production note

The exploratory files above remain preserved. Faithful raster masters, transparent
size exports, usage proofs, a small-size proof, and reproducible build and validation
scripts live in `finalized/`. The rejected vector reconstruction is not approved and
must not be used. If vector artwork is needed later, trace the approved raster
masters with continuous overlay comparison rather than starting from the rejected
SVGs. Conduct a formal clearance review before trademark registration or commercial
launch.
