# Nelson Grey developer colorway

The developer edition is a contextual colorway of the official Nelson Grey icon, not a replacement logo or a redrawn mark.

## The change

The repository's `assets/nelson-grey-mark.svg` is the Core source of truth. Every internal path, stroke width, endpoint, proportion, spacing decision, and rounded-square dimension remains identical in the developer treatment.

The field and `N` colors change; the Core-only contrast keyline is not needed on the dark developer field:

| Element | Official core icon | Developer colorway |
|---|---|---|
| Field | `#F8F4EA` | `#101722` |
| `N` | `#101722` | `#F8F4EA` |
| Teal arc and endpoint | `#08756F` | Unchanged |
| Gold details and endpoint | `#C9952F` | Unchanged |
| Outer keyline | `#D7CCB8` | Not used; the dark field defines its own edge |

No internal segment, badge, glyph, label, or effect is added.

## The meaning

The identity stays visibly Nelson Grey. The dark field places the familiar mark in a focused build environment, while the ivory `N` restores contrast. Keeping the teal and gold intact preserves the movement and character of the icon the audience already recognizes.

The cue is environmental rather than literal. It avoids adding `DEV`, code brackets, terminal prompts, gears, or a second badge. The result communicates software development through context while retaining the meaning and equity of the official mark.

## Usage

Use the developer colorway for:

- Google Play Developer Profile
- engineering or developer-community profiles
- technical presentations and repository organization artwork
- developer-focused social or channel avatars

Use the official core mark for:

- the Nelson Grey company and portfolio site
- general social profiles
- press and email identity
- legal or company-level presentation

Do not use either Nelson Grey mark as an individual product's app icon.

## Naming system

Use `Nelson Grey` as the public brand and `Nelson Grey LLC` where legal identity matters. In visual identity lockups, use the canonical raised `LLC` suffix from `nelson-grey-name-lockup.svg` or its reverse source. In prose and platform fields, keep the legal name inline. The developer-page label is `Software & apps`, but it remains page copy or metadata and is never attached to the logo. Do not create tagline or descriptor lockups.
