Nelson Grey LLC created Modulo Squares to turn divisibility into clear, tactile, replayable play. Players guide falling numbers into buckets that divide them evenly, build combos, fill a 10 × 10 progress grid, and adapt as the pace accelerates. Optional visual cues make the core idea approachable without replacing the player's decision. The app supports guest play, platform-appropriate sign-in, a local best score, free ad-supported sessions, and a one-time Premium purchase to remove ads. We would welcome consideration for featuring around puzzle games, number games, learning through play, or stories about developers translating familiar ideas into polished interactions.
