Modulo Squares was designed, engineered, tested, and supported around one principle: explain the rule clearly, then let play create the challenge. The falling-number interface, shuffled divisor buckets, optional cues, accelerating timer, combo feedback, and 10 × 10 progress grid are parts of one coherent learning-through-play loop.
