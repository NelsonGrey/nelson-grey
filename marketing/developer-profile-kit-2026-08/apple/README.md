# Apple developer-presence package

Apple's public App Store developer page is generated from catalog data. There is no comparable account-level upload for the Nelson Grey logo, header, bio, email, social links, or website.

## Use this kit on Apple through

1. `https://nelsongrey.com/` or an appropriate live Nelson Grey portfolio page used as each app's Marketing URL.
2. Product-specific Support URLs with real, monitored contact information; use `hello@nelsongrey.com` for public company contact where appropriate.
3. Consistent app icons and product-page media within each app's own identity.
4. Custom product pages for distinct audiences and campaign stories.
5. Product-page optimization for icon, screenshot, and preview decisions.
6. In-App Events for genuine time-bound live events, premieres, challenges, competitions, new seasons, major updates, or special events.
7. Featuring nominations with a real release/update story and supplemental URLs.
8. Apple's official marketing tools for badges, QR codes, short links, banners, and store-branded social assets.
